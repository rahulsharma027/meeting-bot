Data & privacy

Stored locally

Imported transcripts stay in this local app. Questions, responses and source references are saved in this browser.

Cloud processing

Sources only with Keyword search works locally. When you use Gemini:

  • AI answers send your question and selected passages.
  • Session notes send the selected transcript.
  • Semantic search sends your question; building its index sends transcript passages.

Earlier responses are not sent as chat context. Review generated answers against their sources.

Your controls

Options → Clear conversation removes browser history and saved source references. Imported transcripts stay available. Clearing browser site data also removes conversation history.
